🎯 Goal

Beat the CPU by picking the move that wins. In a match mode, be first to reach the target score. Endless never stops.

📜 Rules

  • ✊ Rock beats ✌️ Scissors
  • ✌️ Scissors beats ✋ Paper
  • ✋ Paper beats ✊ Rock
  • Same move = tie, no points for anyone

🕹️ Controls

  • Tap a move at the bottom, or press R, P, S on a keyboard
  • After a match ends, the buttons restart a fresh match

🧠 CPU levels

  • Random: pure luck, 1/3 chance each
  • Smart: watches your last moves and often counters your favorite one, so mix it up!

🔥 Streak

Consecutive wins count as your streak. A loss resets it to zero, a tie keeps it. Your best streak is saved on this device.
